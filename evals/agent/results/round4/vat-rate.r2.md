# A fact from general knowledge (rep 2)

## Turn 1

The UK standard rate of VAT is **20%**. It has been 20% since 4 January 2011.

- **Adding VAT to a net price:** multiply by 1.2. For example, £1,000 net becomes £1,200 gross.
- **Finding the VAT inside a gross price:** multiply by 1/6. For example, £1,200 gross includes £200 of VAT.
- **Other rates:** some goods and services use the reduced rate (5%) or the zero rate (0%), and some are exempt. Check which category your sale falls into before you apply 20%.
