# A fact from general knowledge (rep 3)

## Turn 1

The standard VAT rate for UK sales is **20%**. It has been 20% since 4 January 2011. The other UK rates are:
- **Reduced rate:** 5% (for example, home energy and children's car seats)
- **Zero rate:** 0% (for example, most food and children's clothes)

So on a standard-rated sale of £100 before VAT, you'd charge £20 VAT, making £120 in total. To get the VAT back out of a price that already includes it, divide by 6: £120 ÷ 6 = £20.

Literacy note: this rate is from my general knowledge, which can be out of date. Check GOV.UK's "VAT rates" page before relying on it.
