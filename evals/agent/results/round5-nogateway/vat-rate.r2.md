# A fact from general knowledge (rep 2)

## Turn 1

The standard rate of UK VAT is **20%**. It has applied to most goods and services since 4 January 2011.

- For a VAT-exclusive net price, VAT is net × 20%. The gross price is net × 1.2.
- For a VAT-inclusive gross price, the VAT part is gross × 1/6, which is the "VAT fraction".

Some goods and services use the reduced rate (5%) or the zero rate (0%) instead, or are exempt. Check that each sale really is standard-rated before applying 20%.

Literacy note: this rate is from my general knowledge, which can be out of date. Check GOV.UK's "VAT rates" page before relying on it.
