# A fact from general knowledge (rep 1)

## Turn 1

The standard rate of UK VAT is **20%**. It has applied to most goods and services since 4 January 2011. There are also two other rates: a reduced rate of 5% (for example, domestic energy) and a zero rate of 0% (for example, most food and children's clothes).

To work out the VAT on a net price, multiply by 0.20. To find the VAT inside a price that already includes it, multiply the gross price by 1/6.
