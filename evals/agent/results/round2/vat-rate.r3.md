# A fact from general knowledge (rep 3)

## Turn 1

Standard-rated UK sales are charged VAT at **20%**. The rate has been 20% since 4 January 2011.

- To add VAT to a net price, multiply it by 1.20.
- To find the VAT inside a gross price, divide the gross by 6. For example, £120 gross is £100 net plus £20 VAT.
- The other UK rates are 5% (reduced) and 0% (zero-rated). Some sales are exempt from VAT altogether.
