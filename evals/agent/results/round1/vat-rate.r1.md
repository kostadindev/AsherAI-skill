# A fact from general knowledge (rep 1)

## Turn 1

The UK standard VAT rate is **20%**. It has been 20% since 4 January 2011. The other rates are the reduced rate of 5% (for example, domestic energy) and the zero rate of 0% (most food and children's clothes).

If you have a VAT-inclusive price, the VAT is 1/6 of it, because 20/120 = 1/6.
