# A fact from general knowledge (rep 3)

## Turn 1

Standard-rated UK sales are charged VAT at **20%**. That has been the rate since 4 January 2011.

- **Adding VAT to a net price:** net × 20%. For example, £1,000 net plus £200 VAT is £1,200 gross.
- **Working out the VAT inside a gross price:** gross × 1/6. For example, £1,200 gross contains £200 VAT.
- **Other rates:** the reduced rate is 5% and the zero rate is 0%. Some sales are exempt or outside the scope of VAT, so check which category a sale falls into before using 20%.

Literacy note: this rate comes from my general knowledge, not a source you gave me, and rates can change. Check GOV.UK ("VAT rates") before you rely on it.
