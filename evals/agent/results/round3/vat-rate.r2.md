# A fact from general knowledge (rep 2)

## Turn 1

The UK standard rate of VAT is **20%**. It has been 20% since 4 January 2011, and it covers most goods and services.

- The reduced rate is 5%, for things like home energy and children's car seats.
- The zero rate is 0%, for most food, books and children's clothes.
- If a price includes VAT, the VAT part is 1/6 of the total. For example, £120 including VAT is £100 plus £20 VAT.
