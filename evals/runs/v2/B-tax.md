**The UK corporation tax main rate is 25%.** It applies to companies with profits over £250,000, so yes, a company with £300,000 of taxable profit pays the main rate on all of it.

- **Tax due:** £300,000 × 25% = **£75,000**. The 25% applies to the whole amount, not just the part above £250,000. Marginal relief only covers profits between £50,000 and £250,000.
- **Other bands:** profits up to £50,000 pay the small profits rate of 19%. Between £50,000 and £250,000, marginal relief gives an effective rate between the two.

**When it could be different:**
- **Associated companies:** the £50,000 and £250,000 limits are divided by the number of associated companies. That only strengthens the main-rate answer here.
- **Short accounting periods:** the limits are reduced in proportion for periods shorter than 12 months. This also only pushes £300,000 further into the main rate.
- **Augmented profits:** the thresholds are tested on taxable profit plus dividends received from companies outside the group. That can only raise the figure, so the conclusion holds.
- **Year:** these rates have applied since 1 April 2023. Make sure the accounting period you're dealing with falls under the same rates.
